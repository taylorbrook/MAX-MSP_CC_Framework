## Key Findings (Top 3)

1. **FN-01 / DQ-02 (critical silent bug, fixed):** 5 objects had `variable_io: true` with per-domain inline `io_rule` but no `overrides.json:variable_io_rules` entry. `compute_io_counts` silently ignored them. Lifted 3 of the 5 (`cycle`, `combine`, `router`) into the loaded registry; 2 remain (`routepass`, `o.route`) as judgment-required.
2. **DQ-01 (moderate, fixed):** `wrap~` blocklist entry misleads users of MAX's own `wrap~` (which exists with a different purpose than PD's). Rewrote to match the `clip~` both-exist disambiguation pattern.
3. **DQ-04 (cosmetic, deferred):** All 560 rnbo-domain entries lack `rnbo_compatible: true`. This causes 300 of 301 cross-domain duplicates to show as "schema mismatches". Convention-level — needs user decision to flip or document.
