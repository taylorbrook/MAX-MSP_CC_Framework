These were resolved during research and orchestration. **Do not re-litigate them.**

**S1 — The task's `variable_io` premise is WRONG and must NOT be implemented.**
The originating task text says "handle node.script's configurable outlet count via variable_io". Five independent bundle sources say the object is **fixed 1 inlet / 2 outlets**: the refpage declares exactly two `<outlet>` elements; the sole documented argument is a required `symbol` (a filename, not a count); no outlet-count attribute appears in the refpage or in the binary's string table; all 24 `node.script` instances in Cycling '74's own shipped patchers carry `numoutlets: 2`; and the anatomy vignette names "the left outlet" and "the rightmost outlet". The "configurable outlets" phrase traces to this repo's own `add_node_script(num_outlets=2)` builder, not to Max.

Therefore: write `variable_io: false`, fixed I/O, **no** `variable_io_rules` entry, **no** new `io_rule` formula. A rule here would be actively harmful — `SUPPORTED_IO_FORMULAS` is validated at DB construction and raises `ValueError` for unknown names, `first_arg` against a filename triggers `_warn_non_integer_first_arg` on every lookup, and `fixed:2` would misclassify the object into `audit_empty_io()["variable_io_ok"]`. Do not "fix" this back. The SUMMARY must call this deviation out prominently.

**S2 — Placement is `.claude/max-objects/max/objects.json` (core), not `packages/`.**
11 of 13 project configs read `packages: []`, and `lookup()` filters package-resident objects whenever `allowed_packages` is not `None` — a `packages/Node for Max/` entry would keep the Rule #1 error alive for those 11 projects. Core placement also avoids the `package`-field schema tax. Precedent: `v8`, `v8.codebox`, `js`, `jweb`, `dict.view` are all core-resident, and `node.script`'s own `<seealso>` peers are already there.

**S3 — Curation deltas C1–C4 apply** (the extractor cannot make these):
- C1: add `rnbo_compatible: false` to both (`parse_standard_xml` never sets it; all 471 neighbors have it).
- C2: replace the inlet `digest` placeholder with curated text paraphrasing the refpage's own `<description>`. Zero of the 471 existing entries carry a template placeholder digest — shipping one is a visible regression.
- C3: `category: "Languages"` on both, matching `js` / `v8` / `v8.codebox`.
- C4: `node.codebox.arguments` = `[]`. Its refpage `objarglist` is a verbatim copy of `node.script`'s (the whole refpage is a near-clone); the real object embeds its JS in a `code` attribute and the shipped help-patch instance has no `text` and no args.

**S4 — OMIT the undocumented attributes.** `options` (both objects) and `code` (`node.codebox`) are bundle-verified but refpage-undocumented.
