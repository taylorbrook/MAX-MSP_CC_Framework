## Decisions Made
- COLLISION_PAD overlap uses half-pad expansion per rect (Minkowski sum approach) so 5px means 2.5px expansion per side, matching the intent that boxes need at least 5px total clearance
- Comment and panel boxes are excluded from overlap checks since they are decorative background elements that intentionally overlap content
- Horizontal offset midpoint check only applies to downward/same-level cables; upward cables use the separate UPWARD_BUS_THRESHOLD check
