## 5. Two-octave glide in 100 ms across the limiter (bell, 1200 -> 4800 Hz, sr 96000)

max |literal (64-sample cursor refresh) - ideal (per-sample weights)| = 2.78e-02 (-31 dBFS). This is fade LAG, not a step: a weight is at most 64 samples old, in which this glide moves f by 0.93 %, so a partial the stale weight still passes sits below 21801 Hz -- under Nyquist at every supported rate (22050+). Slower glides / vibrato lag proportionally less.
